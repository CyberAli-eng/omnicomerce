# Getting Started with OmniCommerce

## Welcome! 👋

This 5-minute guide will get you up and running with OmniCommerce.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account (1 minute)

1. Visit your OmniCommerce  dashboard
2. Click **"Sign up"**
3. Enter:
   - Your name
   - Business email
   - Password
4. Click **"Create Account"**

✅ **You're in!**

---

## Step 2: Connect Your Shopify Store (2 minutes)

### Quick Method (OAuth)

1. Click **"Integrations"** in the sidebar
2. Find **Shopify** card
3. Click **"Connect via OAuth (Recommended)"**
4. Enter your shop domain (e.g., `mystore`)
5. Authorize on Shopify
6. Done! ✅

### Alternative Method (Manual)

1. Get your Shopify Admin API token:
   - Shopify Admin → Settings → Apps → Develop apps
   - Create app → Configure scopes → Install → Copy token

2. In LaCleoOmnia:
   - Click **"Connect Manually"**
   - Enter shop domain and token
   - Click **"Connect"**

---

## Step 3: Import Your Orders (1 minute)

1. On the **Integrations** page
2. Find your connected Shopify store
3. Click **"Import Orders"**
4. Wait for success message
5. Go to **Orders** page to see your orders

✅ **Orders imported!**

---

## Step 4: Process Your First Order (1 minute)

1. Go to **Orders** page
2. Click **"View"** on any order
3. Click **"Confirm"** → Order is confirmed
4. Click **"Pack"** → Order is packed
5. Click **"Ship"** → Select courier (e.g. Delhivery, Selloship) and enter AWB or generate label

✅ **Order shipped!**

---

## You're All Set! 🎉

### What's Next?

- **Explore Dashboard**: See your business overview and profit summary
- **More channels**: Integrations → use the **Guide** button on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Delhivery, Selloship for step-by-step setup
- **Connect couriers**: Integrations → Logistics → Delhivery / Selloship (paste API key) for tracking and RTO
- **Manage Inventory**: Set up stock levels; sync from Shopify
- **Set SKU costs**: Costs page → add product/packaging costs for accurate profit
- **Generate Labels**: Create shipping labels (Delhivery, Selloship, etc.)
- **View Analytics**: Check revenue, net profit, margin, and RTO/loss
